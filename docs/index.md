AutoLogger is a logging workspace for live productions: podcasts, shoots and streams. Operators
press a key or a Stream Deck button and the moment lands on the session's timecode. Recorded or
imported audio becomes a speaker-labelled transcript on the same clock. AI agents you connect over
MCP add chapters, events, dashboards and a show wiki, as you, and every change they make can be
undone.

## Where to start

- **Running a show?** Start with [Getting started](/docs/guide/getting-started), then
  [Sessions and logging](/docs/guide/sessions-and-logging).
- **Bringing an AI agent?** [Connecting agents](/docs/agents/connecting-agents) gets Claude Code,
  claude.ai or another MCP client signed in.
- **Hosting or changing AutoLogger?** See [Self-hosting](/docs/developers/self-hosting) and
  [Contributing](/docs/developers/contributing).
